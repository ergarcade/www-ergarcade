Below is a description of how the data for these charts was assembled.

## Method

Data for the Concept2 BikeErg was collected in the same manner as
[described in this article](/articles/bikeerg-damper-pace-derivatives/). The Schwinn AirDyne6, Assault bike and Rogue Echo
data were collected by video recording the respective manufacturers'
monitors at increasing RPM.

## Setup

- The BikeErg used in testing was brand new.
- The Schwinn AirDyne6 was quite a few years old. However, it has had regular maintenance, including belts being replaced prior to testing.
- The Assault bike was less than 6 months old.
- The Rogue Echo was a couple of years old.
- Flywheel cages had dust and debris brushed out.

## Collation

Data was collated in the same manner as [described
previously](/articles/bikeerg-damper-pace-derivatives/).

## Caveats

The graphs presented above are based on results recorded on a single
machine. Your may see different results on your own devices due to:

- Different amount of flywheel dust or debris which alters drag factor;
- Air pressure differences, notably from differing altitudes, again impacting drag factor;
- Differing amounts of wear on machines.
